# Competitive Intel — CloudBox99

## Market Positioning
A low-cost VPS provider with included 24/7 support, for startups that find the
hyperscalers expensive and their support slow or paid.

## Our Unique Strengths
- About 50% cheaper than comparable AWS instances
- 24/7 support included on every plan, at no extra cost
- 7-day free trial

## vs. AWS (also applies to Google Cloud and Azure)
- **Their strength:** huge range of managed services, global regions, startup credits.
- **Our angle:** for plain servers (app servers, staging, databases on a VM,
  internal tools), you pay roughly half, and support is free instead of a paid plan.
- **Be honest:** if they rely on AWS-only services, don't push a full move —
  suggest moving one plain server first.

## vs. DigitalOcean, Linode, Vultr, Hostinger and other VPS hosts
We have no verified price comparison against these. Don't claim to be cheaper
than them. If asked, point to the free 24/7 support and offer the free trial
so they can compare for themselves.

## Rules
- The "50% cheaper" claim is against AWS only.
- Never criticise a competitor's reliability or quality.
