## What We Sell
Cloud VPS servers, Linux and Windows, sold by CloudBox99 (cloudbox99.com).
The website also lists dedicated servers, cloud storage, managed hosting and
backup — mention these only if the prospect asks, and pass detailed questions
to the team.

## Core Value Proposition
Run your servers at roughly half of what AWS charges, with free 24/7 support
included, so a small engineering team isn't stuck babysitting infrastructure
or paying for a premium support plan.

## Key Benefits
- About 50% cheaper than comparable AWS instances
- Free 24/7 support on every plan
- 99% uptime guarantee
- Simple monthly pricing in rupees

## Pricing
- Linux VPS: from Rs.899/month
- Windows VPS: from Rs.1,299/month
- 7-day free trial for new customers

Exact plan specs (CPU, RAM, storage) for each price are not in this file — if
asked, say you'll send the plan sheet.

## Target Audience
Indian tech startups and SaaS companies, roughly 5-200 people, in Bengaluru,
Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Gurugram, Noida and Mumbai.

## Ideal Buyer Persona
The CTO, technical co-founder or engineering lead who owns the cloud bill and
gets the call when a server goes down. Cares about: monthly burn, not being
woken up at 3am, and not losing a week to a migration.

## Prospect Pain Points
- AWS / GCP / Azure bill growing faster than revenue; credits running out
- Paying extra for a support plan, or getting slow ticket replies
- A small team spending engineering time on server maintenance
- Hiring a DevOps engineer mainly to keep servers running

## Buying Triggers
- Hiring a DevOps engineer, cloud engineer, SRE or sysadmin
- AWS startup credits ending (often 12-24 months after funding)
- Recently raised a round and scaling infrastructure
- Launching a new product or environment (staging, internal tools)

## Disqualifiers
- Large enterprises with dedicated cloud teams and long procurement
- Companies deeply built on AWS-only managed services (Lambda, DynamoDB, etc.)
  — a VPS is not a drop-in replacement for those
- Non-technical businesses with no servers of their own

## What Makes Us Different
Price (about half of AWS) plus free 24/7 support. Lead with the saving and
the support; do not invent other differentiators.

## The Offer
A 7-day free trial. Suggest starting with one non-critical server (staging,
an internal tool, a side project) so there is no risk to production. If they
want to talk first: a 15-minute Microsoft Teams call — propose two or three
specific times in IST (no booking link yet).
